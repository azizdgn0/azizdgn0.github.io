Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

WWD Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the World Works Daily YouTube channel to upload and schedule his own videos.

What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and publishing schedule, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What the app does with data

The app does not collect, store, sell, share or analyse personal data from any other person. The only credential it holds is the owner's own access token, kept in a private encrypted secret store and never published.

Google and YouTube

The app's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
